Pactovio

The pre-sales engine for technical B2B suppliers. Every tender, RFP and design request, answered with proof.

  • Finds public tenders that fit your company and reads the whole pack.
  • Says bid or no-bid for each lot, with the page every reason comes from.
  • Drafts answers only from your own evidence and fills the buyer's own forms.
  • Matches customer design requests against your datasheets and prices the quote in code.
  • Connects to the tools your team already uses: email, chat, CRM, files, calendar, project tools, e-signature and accounting.

Built on NVIDIA Nemotron models served by Nebius Token Factory. The full app is launching here shortly.
