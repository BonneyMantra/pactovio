The service

Pactovio is provided as is, currently as a preview. It helps you prepare tender responses, proposals and quotes; you remain responsible for reviewing and approving everything before it is submitted or sent. Pactovio flags claims it cannot support with your evidence, but it does not guarantee the outcome of any bid.

Your accounts and material

You may only connect accounts and upload material you are entitled to use. Paid plans are billed through Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, under its terms. The sample companies Harbour Digital and Kestrel Sensing shown in the preview are fictional.
