Questions.Answered plainly.

What counts as a credit?

Only the expensive work: reading a tender pack (1 credit up to 120 pages, 2 up to 400, 4 above that), a design match (1) and a buyer refresh (1). Email triage, agent chat, quotes, connector actions and replays of a finished read don't use credits.

What happens when I run out?

An action that needs credits stops with a clear "not enough credits" message and nothing is charged. Buy a 100-credit pack or move to a bigger plan, and carry on. If a pack read fails partway, its credits are returned automatically.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. Create your private workspace with an email code, add your company details and start on Scout. Connecting your email is optional. Billing currently runs in test mode, so there are no real charges.

How do I switch plans?

From Billing & usage in the app. Upgrades start a new checkout; your remaining credits stay in your workspace.

Who handles payment?

Dodo Payments is the merchant of record and handles checkout, tax and receipts. The preview runs in Dodo's test mode, so no real card is charged.
